Approach

The method follows recognised project-controls practice, including AACE International's Total Cost Management framework, and is written for the physics of solar and storage construction: feeders, blocks, quantities installed, and systems commissioned.

For owners and developers, it means assurance and early warning against a schedule built to a clear specification. For EPC contractors, it means daily progress capture, productivity and forecasting from the same model.
